Privacy Policy for LumaGlass Widgets

Effective Date: November 2026

LumaGlass Widgets is designed as an offline-first, privacy-respecting Android widget application. We do not require user accounts, login credentials, or personal cloud profiles.

1. Local-Only Widget Data

All widget customizations — including transparency, colors, countdown dates, habit streak counters, sticky notes, and selected photos — are stored strictly on your device using Android Preferences DataStore and app-private storage. We do not operate any backend server and never upload your notes or photos.

2. Google AdMob & Play Billing

The free tier uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded ads (to unlock additional widgets) and occasional interstitial ads. AdMob may collect standard device identifiers, IP address, and diagnostic ad-performance telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional one-time Pro purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card or UPI details.

3. Device Permissions

  • Wi-Fi / Network State: Read locally on-device solely to display Wi-Fi connectivity status in the Quick-Glance Status widget.
  • Photo Picker: Grants one-time read access to only the specific image(s) you select for your photo widgets.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries or support, contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
